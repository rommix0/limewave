## Known limitations

These are known and deliberately left for a later version.

- A low drum whose pitch falls (such as `oneshot.wav`) is not detected as tonal, and its tails fade more slowly than the real ending, because no fit on the audio before the cut can know that the decay speeds up. Set the decay time by ear.
- On the real `0060_020274_0ACB.wav` sample, the Harmonic sustain body measured a 3.58 dB level spread (Granular 1.00 dB, Smooth 0.56 dB). Listen before relying on Harmonic for sustained loops.
- Spectrum loops at high sample rates and very low pitches take a few seconds to render. They render in the background.
- A tail or spectrum loop is stored as the audio it rendered. To change its settings, undo it and apply again.
- Extra `smpl` loops that touch audio a tail or spectrum loop replaced cannot be kept; the save dialog lists them.
- Redo of a tail or spectrum loop is covered only by the shared history tests, not by a test of its own.
- The v0.2 limitations are unchanged (see the v0.2 checklist): cue markers are kept but not shown, the envelope is for audition only, a backward loop has no crossfade, many samplers ignore backward loops, and only one release tail survives rapid file switching.

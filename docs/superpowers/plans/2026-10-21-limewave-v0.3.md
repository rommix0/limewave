# Limewave v0.3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build Limewave v0.3 on top of v0.2. It adds:
- **Generate tail…**: Decay and Sustain modes, with Smooth, Harmonic and Granular methods
- **Make loop from spectrum…**: a separate effect with entry and exit transitions
- the **Release plays after loop end** audition option
- the `tail_lab` test bench
- the groundwork all of this needs: effects that change the file's length

**Architecture:**
- **A new kind of effect step: a splice.**
  - Both new features are effects that replace a frame range with new audio. That makes them the first length-changing steps in `EditState.effects`.
  - The step stores the finished replacement audio (in an `Arc`), so replay is a cheap splice and always gives the same result.
  - The core learns per-step lengths, frame mapping that can drop or shift frames, and saving with a changed length.
- **New library modules.** These all live in `limewave-core` and are pure (no UI or audio-device code):
  - DSP building blocks: FFT, windows, envelope, pitch, least squares, seeded random numbers;
  - `tail` (with the three methods);
  - `spectrum_loop`.
- **The app** gets:
  - length-aware views and a tint over generated audio;
  - a background render worker;
  - dialog previews played through their own play buffer;
  - the new audition option.

**Tech Stack:**
- Rust (edition 2024)
- eframe/egui 0.37 (with `persistence`)
- cpal 0.18, midir 0.11, rfd 0.17, arc-swap 1.9, rtrb 0.4
- thiserror 2, tempfile 3
- **new:** `realfft` (MIT OR Apache-2.0; it brings in `rustfft`)
- egui_kittest 0.37 (dev)

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-limewave-tails-design.md`, cited below as "v0.3 §N". It builds on the v0.2 and v0.1 designs. Read v0.3 in full before starting any task.

**Plan-level rulings** (recorded here because they refine the spec):
- **R1. A generated step stores its finished audio.**
  - The spec says a tail stores a resolved plan (v0.3 §4.9), and a spectrum loop stores settings plus a seed (v0.3 §6). Instead, a splice step stores the replacement audio it rendered.
  - **Why this is safe:** history only ever pushes onto, or rewinds, a linear list. A step's input is therefore always identical when it is replayed, so its stored output stays valid.
  - **What it gains:** replay never searches or re-analyses, and there is less code.
  - **Cost if wrong:** memory, about 11 MB for a 30 s stereo 48 kHz tail. That is shared between history entries through `Arc`.
  - The step still records its kind and settings, for the tint, the dialogs and the tests.
- **R2. Dialog previews get their own envelope behaviour.**
  - Dialog previews play through a new `Command::PreviewOn`. That voice uses the session's attack, plays past the loop end on release, and holds its level through the release instead of fading.
  - This is how "attack but not release" (v0.3 §7.1) is met without touching the session envelope.

## Global Constraints

- **Licence:** `MIT OR Apache-2.0`. Dependencies must be permissively licensed, and `cargo deny check licenses` stays clean.
- **No product names:** never name other commercial audio products in code, comments, UI text, docs or commit messages.
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Linux. No platform-specific code beyond what crates handle.
- **Audio callback:** it never allocates, locks or blocks.
- **Bad input:** library code never panics on it. Errors are values, and lenient fixes are reported as warnings.
- **`smpl` loops:** the loop end is inclusive. Types: 0 forward, 1 ping-pong, 2 backward. Types 3 and up are kept until the loop is edited.
- **Frame count:** effects keep it, except the two splice steps:
  - **Generate tail** replaces from the start of its join overlap to the end of the file;
  - **Make loop from spectrum** replaces `a … b + T`.

  Channel tools change only the channel count (v0.3 §6).
- **Pipeline:** original audio → effects in order (including splices) → crossfade → trim.
- **Same results every time:** the same input and settings always give the same samples. Randomness comes only from the seeded `dsp::rng::SplitMix64`.
- **No v0.2 regressions:** a file with no splice step behaves and saves exactly as in v0.2. A save with no edits gives back the original bytes, apart from `ISFT`.
- **Values from the spec:**
  - **Analysis** (v0.3 §4.2):
    - Steady when the whole-region slope is shallower than −3 dB/s.
    - The decay rate is fitted over the most recent 40 % of the region, at least 50 ms, and is never shallower than the whole-region slope.
    - Character: autocorrelation over 40 Hz–2 kHz, searched after the first dip below zero, preferring the shortest peak within 90 % of the best. Tonal threshold 0.6 for tails, 0.45 for spectrum loops.
    - Level at the cut: RMS of the last 20 ms.
    - Spectral frames: about 93 ms rounded down to a power of two, with at least 2.5 frames in the region, Hann windows, a hop of a quarter frame, and the last frame ending at the cut.
    - Per-bin rates: fitted over the last 8 frames, smoothed over 9 bins, and limited to 0.5×–4× the overall rate.
  - **Source region** (v0.3 §4.1):
    - The selection, or the last 1 s before the cut, never including the first 20 % of Start…cut.
    - Minimum 30 ms, and 4 periods for tonal sources.
  - **Decay** (v0.3 §3.1):
    - Default 1.0 s to −60 dB when the source is steady.
    - The tail ends at −90 dBFS or after 30 s, then ramps to zero over 5 ms.
    - Darken: 0–100 %, default 0.
  - **Sustain** (v0.3 §3.2):
    - Body 2.0 s by default, range 0.1–30 s.
    - Release default: the fitted decay time, or 0.5 s when the source is steady. 0 means no release.
  - **Granular** (v0.3 §4.3):
    - Flattening window: 20 ms, or 2 periods if that is longer.
    - Patch: tonal ~40 ms in whole periods (at least 4 periods); noisy 50 ms, or 2× the strongest repeat if that is longer.
    - Overlap: tonal whole periods adding up to at least 5 ms; noisy 25 %.
    - Pick at random among the best 3 distinct candidates, with a reuse penalty.
    - Crossfades: linear for tonal, equal-power for noisy.
    - Darken: a low-pass reaching 500 Hz at −60 dB at 100 %.
  - **Smooth and Harmonic** (v0.3 §4.4–4.5):
    - Peak locking ±2 bins.
    - A peak must be more than 12 dB above the local median and within 70 dB of the loudest peak.
    - Harmonic: at most 60 partials, each found within ±¼ of the fundamental of its expected place.
    - Darken: the rate is multiplied by up to (1 + 3 × Darken) at the Nyquist frequency, rising linearly with frequency.
    - Join: a linear crossfade over the last quarter frame.
  - **Join next to an existing loop** (v0.3 §4.6): the overlap never reaches the loop end. Its minimum is one period, or 2 ms for noisy sounds.
  - **Spectrum loop** (v0.3 §5):
    - FFT size: 256–65536, powers of two. Auto picks the largest power of two that is ≤ the source length ÷ 1.5 and ≤ 186 ms.
    - Detune 0–50 cents, default 0.
    - Loop 0.1–30 s, default 3.0 s.
    - Transition 10–1000 ms, default 100 ms, and at most half the source range.
    - Ensemble noise floor −60 dB. Peak limited to −0.2 dBFS.
    - Steady amplitude window: 4 periods or 50 ms, whichever is longer.
  - **UI** (v0.3 §7):
    - Re-plan or re-render after a 200 ms pause.
    - Tail preview starts 1 s before the cut.
    - "Release plays after loop end" is off by default and remembered between runs.
    - The generated-audio tint is lime `#a3e635`.
- **Version:** `0.3.0`, so the software tag reads `Limewave 0.3.0` (Task 14).
- **Every task ends with these clean:**
  - `cargo fmt --check`
  - `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`
  - `cargo test --workspace --all-features`

## Review Focus

These are the inputs and failure modes most likely to bite a user first. Each one has a pinned test in the task that owns the code.

1. **Undo or redo across a tail while the view, the selection or the play-from point lie beyond the restored end.** They must be clamped. Nothing may panic or draw out of range. *Task 9: `undo_of_a_tail_clamps_view_selection_and_play_from`.*
2. **A silent, too-short or one-frame source.** The result is an error value, with no NaN or infinity and no panic. *Task 4: `silent_or_short_source_is_refused`. Task 7: `spectrum_loop_of_silence_is_refused`.*
3. **Stereo with channels in opposite phase.** The analysis must not cancel, and the tail must keep the channel correlation. *Task 3: `out_of_phase_stereo_is_measured_by_energy`. Task 4: `granular_keeps_stereo_correlation`.*
4. **Saving after a tail.** Covers 24-bit and 32-bit float files, cues past the cut, an end-of-file cue, an `ltxt` range crossing the cut, and extra `smpl` loops. *Task 2: `tail_save_reencodes_every_format`, `cues_follow_a_splice` and `extra_loops_touching_replaced_audio_are_reported`.*
5. **The user edits while a render is still running.** The stale result must be thrown away, never applied over the newer edit. *Task 11: `stale_render_is_discarded`.*

### Task 1: Splice steps and the length-aware edit model

**Files:**
- Modify: `crates/limewave-core/src/effects.rs`
- Modify: `crates/limewave-core/src/edit.rs`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces (in `limewave_core::effects`):
  - `pub enum SpliceKind { Tail, SpectrumLoop }` (Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Debug)
  - `pub struct Splice { pub kind: SpliceKind, pub audio: Vec<Vec<f32>>, pub generated: (usize, usize) }` (Clone, PartialEq, Debug).
    - `audio` is the replacement, one Vec per channel, all the same length.
    - `generated` is the inclusive range inside `audio` that is synthetic, used for the tint.
  - `Op::Splice(std::sync::Arc<Splice>)`.
    - Its `AppliedEffect { first, last }` is the **replaced input range**, inclusive.
    - Applying it replaces input frames `first..=last` of every channel with `audio`.
    - When `audio` has a different channel count from the input, the step is skipped and the audio is left alone.
  - `pub fn output_frames(a: &AppliedEffect, input: usize) -> usize`: the input length, unchanged for every op except Splice.
  - `pub fn step_inputs(original: usize, effects: &[AppliedEffect]) -> Vec<usize>`: the input length of each step.
  - `pub fn frames_after(original: usize, effects: &[AppliedEffect]) -> usize`.
  - `pub fn changes_length(effects: &[AppliedEffect]) -> bool`: true when the list holds a Splice.
  - `pub fn map_frame(effects: &[AppliedEffect], f: usize) -> Option<usize>`. **Breaking change: it used to return `usize`.**
    - Reverse mirrors as before.
    - A Splice maps:
      - `f < first` to `f`;
      - `f > last` to `f − (last + 1 − first) + audio_len`;
      - frames inside the replaced range to `None`.
    - `f == input length` (end of file) counts as `f > last`, so it lands on the new length.
  - `pub fn map_range(effects: &[AppliedEffect], first: usize, last: usize) -> Option<(usize, usize)>`:
    - maps both ends;
    - a range crossing a replaced block keeps the part before it if any, otherwise the part after it;
    - Reverse mirrors and reorders;
    - returns `None` when nothing survives.
  - `pub fn generated_ranges(effects: &[AppliedEffect]) -> Vec<(usize, usize)>`: every Splice's synthetic range in final coordinates, mapped through later steps with `map_range`.
- Produces (in `limewave_core::edit`):
  - `EditState::clamped(self, original_frames: usize)` keeps its signature, but now:
    - clamps each effect's range to **its own input length**, from `step_inputs`;
    - drops a Splice whose range no longer fits;
    - clamps markers to `frames_after(original_frames, &effects)`.
  - All callers keep passing the original frame count.
  - `move_marker`, `set_loop_to_selection`, `trim_after_loop` and `crop_to_loop` use the length after effects internally (they take the original count, as today).

### Task 3: DSP building blocks

**Files:**
- Modify: `crates/limewave-core/Cargo.toml` (add `realfft`, latest 3.x)
- Modify: `crates/limewave-core/src/dsp/mod.rs`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces (in `limewave_core::dsp`):
  - `rng::SplitMix64::new(seed: u64)`, `next_u64(&mut self) -> u64`, `next_f32(&mut self) -> f32` (0..1), `phase(&mut self) -> f32` (−π..π).
  - Spectrum:
    - `spectrum::hann(n: usize) -> Vec<f32>`: periodic Hann.
    - `spectrum::Fft::new(n: usize) -> Fft` with `forward(&self, x: &[f32]) -> Vec<Complex32>` (n/2+1 bins) and `inverse(&self, spec: &[Complex32]) -> Vec<f32>`. The inverse is scaled, so `inverse(forward(x)) == x`.
    - `spectrum::frame_size_for(region: usize, rate: u32, target_s: f32, min_frames: f32) -> usize`: the largest power of two ≤ `target_s × rate` with at least `min_frames` frames at a hop of `n/4`.
    - `spectrum::frames_ending_at(ch: &[f32], end: usize, n: usize, first_allowed: usize) -> Vec<Vec<Complex32>>`: Hann frames at a hop of n/4, the last one ending at `end`, oldest first.
    - `spectrum::welch_power(ch: &[f32], first: usize, last: usize, n: usize) -> Vec<f32>`.
  - Envelope:
    - `envelope::rms_db_frames(audio: &[Vec<f32>], first: usize, last: usize, hop: usize) -> Vec<f32>`: mean energy over channels.
    - `envelope::level_db(audio, first, last) -> f32`.
    - `envelope::decay_fit(audio, first, last, rate) -> DecayFit`, where `pub struct DecayFit { pub steady: bool, pub rate_db_s: f32 /* ≥ 0 */, pub t60: Option<f32> }`, using the spec's whole-region and recent rules.
  - `pitch::character(audio, first, last, rate, threshold: f32) -> Character`, where `pub struct Character { pub period: Option<f32>, pub strength: f32, pub strongest_lag: Option<usize> }`.
    - Autocorrelation is summed over channels.
    - The search runs over 40 Hz–2 kHz and starts after the first dip below zero.
    - The shortest peak within 90 % of the best wins.
    - Parabolic interpolation gives a fractional period.
  - `lsq::harmonic_fit(x: &[f32], freqs_hz: &[f64], rate: u32) -> Vec<Complex64>`: the complex amplitude `c` per frequency, so that `x[t] ≈ Re Σ c·e^{iωt}` with `t = 0` at `x[0]`. It solves the normal equations with Cholesky and a 1e-9 ridge, and returns zeros if the system is singular.

## Known limitations

These are known and deliberately left for a later version.

- Every edit, and every drag preview (at most every 50 ms), hands the audio engine a full copy of the file's audio with the crossfade rendered in. For long files this costs memory and time on each update. Sharing the original audio and sending only the crossfaded region is planned.
- Stopping playback, switching files and the Audition toggle cut sounding notes at once instead of fading them out, which can click.
- A fine tune of exactly -50 cents is stored the same way as +50 cents on the note below, so it reopens as that note with +50 cents (the same pitch). +50 cents reopens unchanged.
- Files over 4 GiB (RF64 or longer) are refused with an error.
